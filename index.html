About

I trained as a plant pathologist at the University of the Free State, working on fungal diseases of field crops and the soil microbial communities around crop roots. That work convinced me that the most useful research ends in something a producer can use, and it still shapes how I run trials today.

At Afrikelp I run the applied science programme behind the company's Ecklonia maxima seaweed products: trial design and statistics, data quality, technical support for distribution partners, and training for growers and commercial teams. I also lead the science team.

I grew up partly on the Northern Cape's West Coast, which is where I fell for the natural world. Outside work I spend time in nature, look after my bonsai and grow gourmet mushrooms.
